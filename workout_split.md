## Progression Scheme: Double Progression

1. Start at the **bottom** of the prescribed rep range with your current working weight
2. Each session, aim to add **1 rep** to at least one set
3. Once you hit the **top** of the rep range on **all working sets**, increase weight:
   - Dumbbells: +2 kg per hand
   - Machines: +2.5–5 kg
   - Barbell compounds: +2.5 kg
4. After increasing weight, drop back to the bottom of the rep range
5. Log every session in Hevy — the pipeline will track progressive overload automatically

**Example**: Incline DB Press prescribed 3x8-10
- Week 1: 52kg x 8, 8, 7 → stay at 52kg
- Week 2: 52kg x 9, 8, 8 → stay
- Week 3: 52kg x 10, 10, 9 → stay
- Week 4: 52kg x 10, 10, 10 → increase to 54kg
- Week 5: 54kg x 8, 8, 7 → cycle repeats

## Recomp-Specific Nutrition Notes

Based on your current data (2,000-3,300 kcal daily, 215g avg protein):

- **Training days**: Target maintenance calories or slight surplus (+100-200 kcal). Prioritise carbs around training (pre/post workout meals).
- **Rest days**: Target a mild deficit (-200-300 kcal). Reduce carbs, keep protein and fat stable.
- **Protein**: Maintain 2.2-2.4 g/kg (194-212g/day at 88.3 kg). You're already hitting this consistently.
- **Caloric consistency**: Your current range (2,000-3,300) is too variable. Tighten the band to +-300 kcal from your target on any given day.
- **Weekly net**: Aim for roughly maintenance across the week. The slight surplus on training days fuels muscle protein synthesis; the slight deficit on rest days drives fat oxidation.

## Data-Driven Rationale

### Why these changes vs. your current Coach split?

1. **Glute volume tripled** (from ~6 sets/week estimated to 16). Your quad-to-glute set ratio was 2.6:1 — this brings it closer to 1:1.5 in favour of glutes, which is appropriate for recomp given your current imbalance.

2. **Upper back volume doubled**. At 592 total sets vs. 813 for lats, your pulling was lat-dominant. Adding Iso-Lateral Rows to Upper A and maintaining pull-ups + lat pulldowns on Upper B balances horizontal and vertical pulling.

3. **Lower back added**. Only 92 sets in your entire history. Back extensions on Lower B protect spinal health and support heavier deadlifts and RDLs.

4. **Quad isolation reduced**. Leg extensions dropped from 3-4 sets to 2 sets on Lower A only. Compound leg work (hack squat, leg press) still provides quad stimulus without excessive isolation volume.

5. **Triceps/biceps at maintenance**. These were high volume (1,117 and 816 sets respectively) but aren't limiting factors for recomp. 6 sets/week triceps and 3 sets/week biceps is sufficient when compound pressing and pulling provide additional indirect volume.

6. **Exercise selection preserved**. Every exercise in this plan is one you've already used and tracked in Hevy (except hip thrusts, which are the only new addition). This means your progressive overload data stays continuous.

7. **4 sessions/week target** vs. your recent ~2.5/week average. Consistency is the biggest lever for recomp — aim for all 4 sessions every week.
